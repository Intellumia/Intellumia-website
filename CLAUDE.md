## The one-page rule

Until Intellumia has 5 to 10 paying clients, intellumia.com is **one page with one action, "Book a call"**, plus the Privacy notice. Nothing else is published.

- `docs/index.html` is the page. `docs/privacy/index.html` is Privacy. `docs/404.html` is the not-found page GitHub Pages serves at any unknown address (`noindex`, absolute paths only): "Page not found", "There is nothing at this address.", the headline "Where can AI make you money?" and "Go to intellumia.com" (approved by Sundeep, 3 November 2026; headline from v5.0.6).
- Every other HTML file under `docs/` is a redirect to `https://intellumia.com/`: a meta refresh to `/`, a canonical link to `https://intellumia.com/` and `noindex`. Never delete one: old links must keep landing on the page.
- Retired pages and assets live in `_archive/` at the repository root, outside `docs/`, so GitHub Pages never publishes them. Nothing is deleted; it is moved there, unless Sundeep says to delete it (as with the plant loop, 4 November 2026). (The leaks diagram of Rounds 4 and 4b was inline in the page, so it lives in the git history.)
- A new page, section, line or button needs Sundeep's written approval first.

## The look

The mockup's CSS lives in `docs/assets/site.css`, the one stylesheet: the rules the page uses, in the mockup's order. Keep its breakpoints (600, 700, 768, 800, 900, 1000 and 1100 px) and sizes. The mockup's inline styles are classes in the stylesheet, and its inset shadow on the "Your people" step is a 2 px border (same look, no shadow).

- Colours: white ground `#FFFFFF`, ink `#101010`, ultramarine `#1A1AFF` for buttons, links, the first step, the closing plate and the drawings' lines. Bone `#F3EFE6` for the Map figure, the industry chips, the step drawings and the How it works and Our rules bands. Rule `#DDD8CC`, muted `#5E5B55`. The mockup's two tints: `#2A2926` for second text and `#E6E6FF` for small words on ultramarine. In the drawings, yellow `#FFC800` for money (the coins). In the heat map only, the cyan scale `#E0F8FF`, `#99E6FF` and `#00C2FF` by money at stake. The pictures use the set's five colours: white, ink, rule, ultramarine and vermilion. No dark mode, gradients or shadows.
- Red, vermilion `#FF3D00`. Each picture carries its own one red thing, the point of the picture (Round 8). Outside the pictures, one red thing per screen (Brand System v5.0.7): exactly four on the page, placed so that no two share a screen, and none of them within 200 px of a picture's red (measured at 390 x 844, 1440 x 900 and 1920 x 1080): the hero word "money" (Statement size), the "Often first" tag on the cash card (ink words on vermilion, 5.4:1, so it passes WCAG AA; white would be 3.5:1), the Map's first move (Collections, with its key) and the title of rule 04, "No vendor fees". Nothing else is red: never a button, the plate, Privacy or the 404 page. The social preview carries the same red word.
- Logo: `docs/assets/intellumia-wordmark-ultramarine.svg`, at least 96 px wide, never on a photograph. Favicons: the ink tile with the white ll.
- Type, Anek Latin only, self-hosted from `docs/assets/fonts/` with `OFL-Anek.txt` beside the files: Statement 650 width 80, 48 to 96 px in the centred hero (the plate's question 34 to 64 px); Heading 620, 30 to 52 px; Lead 600, 19 to 22 px; Body 400, 17 to 18 px; Label 600, capitals, +0.12em, 12 to 13 px. Tabular figures; emphasis by colour or weight, never italics. Until Anek has loaded, the text is set in a stand-in already on the device, never downloaded: Arial or its metric twins ('Anek Fallback'), or Roboto on Android ('Anek Fallback Roboto'), one face per weight band, each sized to Anek's widths with its baseline matched (size-adjust, ascent and descent overrides), so lines break the same and the swap moves nothing (Round 7b). It is not a second typeface; re-measure the sizes if the words or the type sizes change much.
- Corners: 6 for buttons, chips and tags; 10 for panels and cards; round for the small portrait and the numbers; 0 for the portrait in "Who you will work with" and for the pictures.
- Pictures (Round 8, Sundeep, 10 November 2026): one picture style, solid ink-and-white shapes on white, one vermilion thing that is the point, ultramarine only for a path or an action. ## Script

`docs/assets/site.js` is the site's own only script, loaded by the page alone. It runs the bottom bar and nothing else: no addresses, no storage, no tracking. Without it the bar stays hidden. The FAQ needs no script (native `details`).

The one third-party script is Cloudflare Web Analytics, cookie-free (Sundeep, 4 November 2026): Cloudflare's snippet (its address `https://static.cloudflareinsights.com/beacon.min.js` and `data-cf-beacon='{"token": "347f1c5c75ff411ead3e9a43282df8cf"}'`), added by one small inline script once the page has loaded and the browser has reported its first contentful paint (straight after load where paint timing is not supported), so it never competes with the first paint (Round 7b, measured): exactly `<script>addEventListener('load',function(){function a(){var s=document.createElement('script');s.src='https://static.cloudflareinsights.com/beacon.min.js';s.setAttribute('data-cf-beacon','{"token": "347f1c5c75ff411ead3e9a43282df8cf"}');document.body.appendChild(s)}var P=window.PerformanceObserver;if(!P||!P.supportedEntryTypes||P.supportedEntryTypes.indexOf('paint')<0)return a();new P(function(l,o){if(l.getEntriesByName('first-contentful-paint').length){o.disconnect();a()}}).observe({type:'paint',buffered:true})})</script>` in the head of Home, Privacy and the 404 page, after the security policy and just before the stylesheet (there it waits for nothing; at the end of the body it held up the first paint by about 40 ms on a throttled phone), and nowhere else. The security policies allow that one inline script by its hash, `'sha256-C5gQQ9Lj4iXDoxOyCRFmNQeWzYj3css48audjxx35fA='`, and nothing else inline (never `'unsafe-inline'` for scripts): Home `script-src 'self' https://static.cloudflareinsights.com 'sha256-C5gQQ9Lj4iXDoxOyCRFmNQeWzYj3css48audjxx35fA='; connect-src 'self' https://cloudflareinsights.com`; Privacy and the 404 page `script-src https://static.cloudflareinsights.com 'sha256-C5gQQ9Lj4iXDoxOyCRFmNQeWzYj3css48audjxx35fA='; connect-src 'self' https://cloudflareinsights.com`. Any change to the loader changes its hash: update the three policies and `check_copy.py` with it. The redirects carry no script. Privacy says in plain words that Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits, sets no cookies, stores nothing on the device and does not track individuals, and that we see only totals. No other analytics.

## Before every pull request

1. `python3 scripts/make_structured_data.py` (writes the structured data from the page and stamps the release date into the page and the sitemap), then `python3 scripts/make_fallback.py` (keeps the root fallback in step with `docs/`).
2. `python3 scripts/check_copy.py`: all checks must pass. Paste the result into the pull request.
3. In a browser at 360, 390, 768, 1100 and 1440 px: no sideways scroll, Anek only, white ground, the four red things and never two on one screen (checked up to 1920 x 1080 and 1440 x 1200; on a 2560 x 1440 monitor two can share, accepted by Sundeep on 4 November 2026), none within 200 px of a picture's red, the pictures sharp at 2x, the header links from 1000 px, the action where The action puts it, the bottom bar's show and hide, the scenes panel (its heading on one line from 1000 px, never overflowing) and the scenes' questions, the heat map (Sales and Finance only below 600 px), the FAQ opening and closing, no decorative motion, the Cloudflare beacon loads with no security-policy errors, every old URL lands on the page, an unknown address shows the 404 page, axe-core finds no WCAG 2.2 AA violations.
4. Lighthouse on Home and Privacy, mobile and desktop. Budgets: LCP 2.0 s on mobile (Home measures about 2.0 to 2.3 s in the lab with the six scenes on its first screen: 1.65 to 2.40 s over ten runs, median about 2.2 s; accepted by Sundeep on 4 November 2026), INP 150 ms (report Total Blocking Time as the lab stand-in), CLS 0.05, under 500 KB on first view (the scenes load their 360 px copies in small slots; the portrait further down is lazy).
5. Screenshots of the whole page at 360 px and 1440 px in the pull request.

Then follow `DEPLOYMENT_AND_ROLLBACK.md`. Open pull requests as drafts. Sundeep merges; nobody else does.
