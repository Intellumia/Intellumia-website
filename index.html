02 — Our businesses

One operating, one in development.

Anthrie

Operating

A fractional AI operating partner for owner-led companies.

Anthrie finds the two or three workflows where AI actually pays, puts them into production through a specialist delivery partner working under its governance, and stays accountable against a baseline set before anything is built. It sells judgment — no licences, no build shop, no headcount to keep billable.

Engagements start with a three-week AI Value Diagnostic: a scored opportunity map, one baselined KPI, a 90-day plan and a straight recommendation — including “do not spend on AI yet” when that is the truth.

Egrysa

In development

Open-source, customer-owned AI egress control.

Egrysa sits between enterprise users and model providers. It removes or blocks high-risk data, routes confidential work to local models, and returns a signed, content-minimised policy receipt — without storing prompt content. The trust boundary runs inside infrastructure the operator owns, which is why the reference implementation is open source.

A security-oriented MVP under Apache-2.0, suitable for technical evaluation in a non-production environment. It is not a certified product, and it does not by itself make an organisation compliant.
