Questions owners ask

Before you book.

Do we need to know where AI fits first?

No. Bring the business and the problem. Finding where AI pays is our job, and sometimes the answer is a simpler fix without AI.

What does a Map cost?

A fixed fee, agreed before we start and always paid. We share it on the first call, once we know the size of your business.

Will it work with our systems?

We start from what you already have: Tally, Excel, your ERP, even paper. The Map needs no new software, and week 1 finds out what data exists.

Is our data safe?

We start with summaries, never all your books on day one. Your data stays in your own environment, with models that neither keep nor train on it.

Will this replace our people?

It is not built to cut jobs. AI takes the routine work, and your people review and decide. The Map shows where time is freed; what you do with it is your call.

Are we tied in after the Map?

No. You keep everything the Map produces. Each move names at least one way to do it without us, and the fee never depends on what you choose next.
