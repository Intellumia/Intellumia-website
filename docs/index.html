Questions owners bring.

If my plant head left tomorrow, what would break first?

Usually the calls nobody wrote down: which order to rush, which supplier to trust. We find those first.

Which of my numbers would move fastest?

The one where a few people’s judgement drives the result and the data already exists. Often follow-up, pricing or purchase timing.

Can the next generation decide the way I do, when I am not in the room?

They can see how you decide, with your reasons, and argue with it. The decision stays theirs.

What should we never hand to AI?

The calls that carry your name: relationships, ethics, the exception that saves a client. AI prepares; people decide.
