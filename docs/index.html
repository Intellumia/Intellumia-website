Four calls that only a few people make

  • The price you hold at launch
  • Which supplier you trust in a shortage
  • The buyer worth a second call
  • When a machine sounds wrong

Each mark is a decision in your business. The lit ones are the few that make your edge. The lime one is where we start.

You can hand over the business. Can you hand over how it decides?

Every system you own records what happened. None of them records why you decided. That lives in a few heads: the maths on the napkin, the instinct for a deal, the reason one supplier gets the call. When those people step back, move on or retire, it goes with them.

The Decision Sprint, then the build
