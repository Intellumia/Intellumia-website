Our point of view

Intelligence is now cheap. Judgement is not.

Every company can buy the same AI. What makes yours different is how a few people decide, and most of that was never written down.

01 / The same AI for everyone

Most companies have spent on AI and seen little back.

In PwC’s 2026 survey of 4,454 chief executives, 56% reported no significant financial benefit from AI yet. The models are not the problem. Where companies start is.

Source: PwC 29th Global CEO Survey, January 2026.

02 / What the platforms read

Every AI platform reads what is written down.

Company “brains” index your systems, documents, email and meetings. That is useful, and it is the same for everyone who buys one. It cannot reach what was never recorded.

Evidence state: our thesis.

03 / Where the edge lives

What runs a business lives in a few people’s heads.

The maths on the napkin, the instinct for a deal, the reason a veteran trusts one supplier. In PwC’s 2026 family business survey, more than a third of Indian leaders had no clear succession plan. Judgement that is not written down leaves when people do.

Source: PwC 12th Global Family Business Survey, 2026 (small Indian sample).

04 / What AI does with it

AI spreads your best people’s judgement to everyone else.

Across 5,179 support agents, AI raised productivity 14% on average and 34% for newer staff, by passing on what the best agents did.

Source: Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond, NBER.

05 / Who should own it

Keep the judgement. Rent the model.

Your judgement is the most valuable thing you could put into an AI system, and the most dangerous to lose. It should live with you, work on any model, and never help a competitor. Our mission: put your best people’s judgement to work on the best intelligence available, and keep it yours.

Evidence state: our promise, in every contract.

What would change our mind

  • If systems that read only written records capture judgement as well as working inside the business does.
  • If controlled studies show captured judgement does not improve results. We measure our own work against a baseline.
  • If owners would rather rent this from a platform than own it.

Bring one decision. Leave with a view.
