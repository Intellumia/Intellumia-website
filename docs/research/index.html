January 2026

Most CEOs have not yet seen AI pay.

56% of CEOs saw neither higher revenue nor lower costs from AI in the past year. 12% saw both.

PwC 29th Global CEO Survey, January 2026. Market evidence, not an Intellumia result.

April 2023

AI spreads the practice of the best people.

An AI assistant raised customer support agents’ issues resolved per hour by 14% on average, and by 34% for the least experienced, by spreading the practice of the best agents.

Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond, “Generative AI at Work”, NBER Working Paper 31161, April 2023. Market evidence, not an Intellumia result.
