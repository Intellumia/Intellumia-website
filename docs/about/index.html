About

Sundeep leads every conversation.

Sundeep Kumar is the founder and CEO of Intellumia. He spent more than two decades at IBM and Microsoft helping large companies across Asia-Pacific change how they work, and now works on the owner’s side of the table.

He reads the numbers himself and stands behind every result. Our AI tools do the heavy analysis.

Why Intellumia exists

To help the businesses that were built on judgement keep their edge as AI changes everything. Our aim is every company operating from its own intelligence. Our work is to put your best people’s judgement to work on the best intelligence available, and keep it yours.

What we hold to

Yours to own. Results before claims. Start small, move fast. Intelligence first, people always. Independent of every tool and model maker.

The company

Intellumia is the judgement engine for owner-led businesses. In five days, on your own data, we find the first move; in 90 days we move one number, and you own what we build.

Intellumia is an AI-native product company, starting with service: we sit beside your team for the first decisions, then engines run them on a layer you own.

Where we are

Intellumia Pte. Ltd., Singapore, is the contracting entity. Sundeep travels to wherever the client is.

Intellumia is pronounced in-tel-LOO-mee-uh.
