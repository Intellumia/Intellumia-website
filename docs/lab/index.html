What we are building, and how far it has got.

Seven pieces that turn what your business knows into something you can use and keep. Each shows its status. None is a finished product yet, and we say so on purpose.

What comes next

Most of the Lab reads what is written down. The next piece goes after what is not.

Email, chat and meetings hold part of what a business knows. The rest lives in a few people’s heads. Draw out is how we will capture it. It is a thesis today, and we will show it here as it moves.
