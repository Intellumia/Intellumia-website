How we workWhere AI works today

We sit on your side of the table.

Your team, your data, your number. One method, four steps, however you work with us.

  1. 1. Sit in

    We start on your side of the table, not across it. Sundeep spends the first days with you and the people who actually run the business: the plant head, the sales lead, the person who signs off every price. We read your numbers in your units, listen more than we talk, and play back what we heard in your words. We arrive with no deck and no answer, only one hypothesis and the questions to test it. The map of where judgement lives in your business is drawn here, with you.

  2. 2. Draw out

    Together we find the handful of decisions where a few people's judgement makes the biggest difference to a number, and we write down what has never been written down: how they decide, on what, and why. We rank the pains by rupee value, urgency and whether the data exists to work on them, and we pick one number worth moving. If no number is worth moving yet, we say so and stop; that is the stop rule, and it is yours to use as much as ours.

  3. 3. Put to work

    We build the first move on your own data, in your environment, and put it into the daily running of the business with your people and on your systems. Sundeep leads; our AI tools do the heavy analysis; your team learns the method as it is built. AI keeps the trace of every decision and its reasons. People still decide. You see the number against its baseline every week, in a one-page note, and an alert reaches you only when something needs a person.

  4. 4. Hand over

    When the number has moved, you decide: go to production, or stop. Either way, what we built stays with you: the method, the data work, the running system and the record of every decision in a layer your business owns. Your own teams or vendors can build it out. We step down as you take it over, and the fee reduces at handover. We come back only if you ask.

Four ways to work with us. No more.
