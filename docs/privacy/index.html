Privacy notice

How we handle your personal data.

In short: this website collects nothing about you. If you contact us, we use what you send only to reply, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Last updated 15 October 2026. Written for India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and Rules 2025, and Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012.

1. Who we are

Intellumia Pte. Ltd., 60 Paya Lebar Road, #07-54 Paya Lebar Square, Singapore 409051, is responsible for the personal data described here. In India’s law we are the data fiduciary; in Singapore’s, the organisation.

Data protection officer and grievance contact: Sundeep Kumar, Founder & CEO, sundeep@intellumia.com. Write to this address for any question, request or complaint about your personal data.

2. What this website collects on its own

Nothing about you, beyond what any web server records. The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and no tracking, and shows no advertising.

Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) records technical details of each visit, such as IP address, browser and the page requested, to keep the service secure. We do not use these records to identify you.

4. Your consent

We use what you send only with your consent, for the purposes above, and for nothing else. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to sundeep@intellumia.com; it is as easy as giving it. We then stop using your data and delete it, unless a law requires us to keep it.

5. Who else handles it

The services listed in section 3 process what you send on our behalf, under their own privacy terms, and may store it outside India or Singapore. We use only providers with comparable protection.

We never sell your personal data, never use it to train AI models, and never share anything that identifies you with another client.

6. How long we keep it

As long as the conversation, and any work that follows, needs it. If nothing follows, we delete it within twelve months of our last contact, unless a law requires otherwise.

7. Your rights

You can ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it, ask us to correct, complete, update or erase it, withdraw consent, and, under India’s law, nominate someone to act for you. We reply within thirty days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India or to Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Commission.

8. Security

We protect personal data with reasonable safeguards, including access controls and encrypted connections. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the regulators as the law requires.

9. Children

This website is for businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under eighteen.
