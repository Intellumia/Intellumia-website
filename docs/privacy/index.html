Privacy notice

How we handle your personal data.

In short: this website collects nothing about you. If you contact us, we use what you send only to reply. Last updated 18 October 2026.

1. Who we are

Intellumia Pte. Ltd., 60 Paya Lebar Road, #07-54 Paya Lebar Square, Singapore 409051. Data protection officer and grievance contact: Sundeep Kumar, sundeep@intellumia.com.

2. What this website collects

Nothing about you beyond what any web server records. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.

3. What you choose to send us

Booking (cal.com): name, email, time, note. WhatsApp: number, name, messages. Email: address, name, what you write. Used only to reply and arrange the conversation.

4. Consent

We use what you send only with your consent. Withdraw it any time by email; it is as easy as giving it. We then delete your data unless a law requires us to keep it.

5. Who else handles it

The services above, under their own terms, possibly outside India or Singapore. We never sell your data, never use it to train AI models, and never share it with another client.

6. How long we keep it

As long as the conversation and any work that follows need it; otherwise deleted within twelve months of our last contact.

7. Your rights

Access, correction, erasure, withdrawal of consent and, under India’s law, nomination. We reply within thirty days. You can complain to the Data Protection Board of India or Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Commission.

8. Security, children, changes

Reasonable safeguards and breach notice as the law requires. Not for anyone under eighteen. When this notice changes, the date changes.
