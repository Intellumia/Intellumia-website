How it works

Find. Prove. Hand over.

One lever in your business, one number to move, built with your team. When it holds, it is yours.

01 · Two to three weeks

First Look

We listen before we build. We sit with you and the few people whose judgement runs the business, and look at how decisions are really made.

What you get

A short written view: where your edge lives, the one lever to start with, the number to move, and what not to do.

02 · Sixty to ninety days

Embed

We work inside your team on one lever. We measure where you start, draw out the judgement that matters, and build with proven pieces.

What you get

A first working build within thirty days, and a measured result against your baseline.

03 · Three to twelve months

Run and improve

We run it with your people until it holds, and improve it as it learns from real decisions.

What you get

A system your people trust and use every day.

04 · When it holds

Hand over

We step back. You own what we built and can run it without us. Then, if you want, the next lever.

What you get

Ownership, in writing, and the option of light support.

What you own at the end

Yours to keep, not ours to rent.

Your data, customer records and recordings.
Your configured system: rules, prompts, scripts and settings.
Your judgement, as we captured it.
The right to run it without us.
What we won’t do

Boundaries, in writing.

Tell you only Intellumia can solve it.
Sell you a deck, a report or a roadmap on its own.
Bill you by the hour or by the head.
Lock you into one model maker or one tool.
Share anything that identifies you with anyone else.
Questions owners ask
Where do you start?

With one lever and one number: the place where a few people’s judgement makes the biggest difference and AI can help fastest.

Do you build everything from scratch?

No. We choose proven tools and models, fit them to your business, and take no fees from their makers.

Which AI models do you use?

Whichever fits the job, from any maker, on tiers that neither keep nor train on your data. You can switch later.

Who will we work with?

Sundeep, with a small senior team. We take on a few businesses at a time.

What happens to our data?

It stays in your accounts wherever possible, is never used to help anyone else, and India’s data protection law is built in from day one.

How do we begin?

A thirty-minute conversation about one lever in your business. You leave with a view either way.
