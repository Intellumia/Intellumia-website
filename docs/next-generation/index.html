You will inherit the business. Will you inherit how it decides?

Ownership passes on paper. How a business decides passes by watching, over years, if anyone has the time.

The shares are the easy part.

The hard part is the judgement behind every big call: why this price, why that supplier, why this customer gets an exception. It lives in the founder's head, and in two or three veterans'. Most of it was never written down.

We write it down with the founder, and put it to work.

We sit in on real decisions, with the founder's permission and in their presence, and ask why. The rules, the examples and the reasons go down in the founder's own words. Then we put them to work on one decision the business makes every week, so the next generation can use them, question them and add to them.

Each decision is kept with its reasons and its result, in one place the business owns. That record, decision by decision, is what we call the layer.

What you own

Your data, the judgement drawn out of your people, the way it is set up for your business, and the record of every decision. You can run it without us, on any model. Nothing we build replaces the founder or the next generation; the decisions stay yours.

Why it compounds

Each decision added starts from the ones before it. What the founder knew becomes what the business knows, and the next generation inherits the reasons along with the results.

Evidence state: our thesis.
