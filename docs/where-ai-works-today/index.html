Where AI works today

What is working, what is coming, and where judgement still decides.

Four industries, seen from the owner’s chair. The results are other companies’ work, credited to their sources. None of them is ours.

Across every industry

AI made newer staff 34% more productive by passing on what the best did.

In a study of 5,179 customer support agents, AI assistance raised productivity by 14% on average and 34% for the least experienced. It worked by spreading the best agents’ practice.

Market story

Source: Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond, Generative AI at Work, NBER.

Real estate

Your best leads are the buyers who already visited.

Working today

AI calling and WhatsApp now handle first contact and follow-up in presales, in Indian languages, around the clock.

Market storySource: Supplier-reported, 2026; we are testing it ourselves
Coming next

Engines that remember every buyer conversation and tell the sales head which ten buyers to call today, and why.

Where judgement still decides

Pricing a launch, reading a serious buyer, knowing when to hold firm.

The question we would ask you
How many buyers who visited the site last quarter never heard from you again?
Lever we would start with: Revenue
Manufacturing

Half of manufacturers already see value. Most say their data is the risk.

Working today

KPMG finds 49% of industrial manufacturers running AI with measurable value, and 76% naming unreliable data as a major risk.

Market storySource: KPMG Global Tech Report 2026
Coming next

Planning and buying that learn from the people who have run the plant for twenty years.

Where judgement still decides

Which order to rush, which supplier to trust in a shortage, when a machine sounds wrong.

The question we would ask you
If your most experienced plant head left tomorrow, which decisions would go wrong first?
Lever we would start with: Margin
Healthcare and diagnostics

AI as a second reader, not a replacement.

Working today

A large Indian hospital network uses AI as a second reader on chest X-rays, more than half its imaging, so subtle findings are not missed.

Market storySource: BioSpectrum India
Coming next

AI drafting reports and clinical notes for a doctor to check and sign.

Where judgement still decides

The clinical call, the hard conversation, the exception to the protocol.

The question we would ask you
Where does a specialist’s time go on work that is not judgement?
Lever we would start with: Efficiency
Law and professional firms

From trials to everyday work, confidentiality first.

Working today

India’s leading firms use AI across research, due diligence and first drafts, with client confidentiality non-negotiable.

Market storySource: Mint, December 2025
Coming next

A firm’s own precedents and partners’ reasoning, searchable by every associate, kept inside the firm.

Where judgement still decides

How a senior partner reads a counterparty, frames a risk, or knows when to settle.

The question we would ask you
What does a senior partner know that a third-year associate cannot look up?
Lever we would start with: People
