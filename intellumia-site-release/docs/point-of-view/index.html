Our point of view

Intelligence is now cheap. Judgement is not.

Every company can buy the same AI. What makes yours different is how a few people decide, and most of that was never written down.

01 / The same AI for everyone

Most companies have spent on AI and seen little back.

In PwC’s 2026 survey of 4,454 chief executives, 56% reported no significant financial benefit from AI yet, and only 12% saw both lower costs and higher revenue. The models are not the problem. Where companies start is.

Source: PwC 29th Global CEO Survey, January 2026.
02 / What the platforms read

Every AI platform reads what is written down.

The new company brains index your systems, documents, email and meetings. That is useful, and it is the same for everyone who buys them. It cannot reach what was never recorded.

Evidence state: thesis, drawn from public descriptions of these products in 2026.
03 / Where the edge lives

What runs a business lives in a few people’s heads.

The maths on the napkin, the instinct for a deal, the reason a veteran trusts one supplier over another. It leaves when they retire, or join a competitor. Some of it can be put into words; some can only be seen by watching decisions being made. That is why we work inside the business, not from outside it.

Evidence state: thesis.
04 / What AI does with it

AI spreads your best people’s judgement to everyone else.

In a study of 5,179 customer support agents, AI assistance raised productivity by 14% on average and by 34% for newer staff, by passing on what the best agents did. Captured judgement does not replace your people. It makes more of them as good as your best.

Source: Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond, Generative AI at Work, NBER.
05 / Who should own it

Keep the judgement. Rent the model.

Your judgement is the most valuable thing you could put into an AI system, and the most dangerous to lose. It should live with you, work on any model, and never help a competitor. We are independent of every model maker for that reason.

Evidence state: our promise, written into every contract.
What would change our mind
If systems that read only written records turn out to capture judgement as well as working inside the business does.
If controlled studies show captured judgement does not improve results. We measure our own work against a baseline to find out.
If owners would rather rent this from a platform than own it.

Test it on your business.
