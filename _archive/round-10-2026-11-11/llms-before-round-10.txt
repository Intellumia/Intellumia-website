One example, step by step, from an overdue invoice to money collected (illustrative, not a client):

1. AI reads the record. Invoice, payments and emails in one view. It spots that the customer asked for a delivery note.
2. AI prepares the next step. It finds the delivery note and drafts the follow-up.
3. Your people decide. Your finance team approves what goes to the customer. A named professional reviews the exceptions.
4. You see the result. Every week: days to collect, overdue balance and the time your team spent.

## The Map

Two to three weeks. Your numbers. Your choice.

Illustration of a Map, not a client: a heat map of eight areas (sales, marketing, service, operations, supply chain, finance, people and compliance), where the darker the cell, the more money at stake. Collections is the first move; passing on costs and quality checks are the next moves.

Every move answers four questions:

1. What could it make you?
2. What would it cost?
3. What needs to be true?
4. How will we measure it?

You see the working. You choose the moves.

- Start: summaries only.
- Week 1: interviews with you and your team.
- Week 2: each move valued and tested on your records.
- Week 3: the readout, to you alone.

About 20 to 40 hours of your staff's time, agreed before we start.
