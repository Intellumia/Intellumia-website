Our rules

On your side of the table, in writing.

  1. 01

    Valued first

    Every move is valued in your numbers before you commit to any build.

  2. 02

    Cash over hours

    Cash counts at close to full value. Hours saved count for little until we name how they will be used.

  3. 03

    Your choice, in writing

    You choose each move, in writing, with at least one way to do it that is not us.

  4. 04

    No vendor fees

    No referral fees and no resale margins, ever. Our advice is paid for by you alone.

  5. 05

    Your data stays put

    In your own environment, with models that neither keep nor train on it.

  6. 06

    Yours to keep

    What we build with you is yours, with the right to run it without us.
